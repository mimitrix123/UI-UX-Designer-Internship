# Competitive Analysis

| Pattern | Google Calendar | Todoist | Apple Health | CareLoop opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scheduling | Strong | Moderate | Moderate | Combine schedule with care context |
| Task ownership | Limited | Strong | Limited | Make family ownership first-class |
| Recurring tasks | Strong | Strong | Moderate | Contextual recurrence with person + purpose |
| Documents | Limited | Limited | Moderate | Dedicated secure document space |
| Family coordination | Limited | Moderate | Limited | Shared timeline and permissions |
| Insights | Basic | Productivity-focused | Strong health metrics | Calm completion insights |
| Privacy controls | General | General | Strong | Explain sharing at the moment of action |

## Competitive takeaway
CareLoop should not compete by becoming a more complex medical record. Its differentiator is **coordination**: the smallest useful layer connecting people, tasks, appointments, reminders, and documents.
