# User Journey Map

**Scenario:** Arjun coordinates a medication task for a family member.

| Stage | Action | Emotion | Friction | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Remembers a recurring task | Concerned | Relies on memory | Clear Today summary |
| Plan | Checks messages/calendar | Overloaded | Sources disagree | One shared timeline |
| Create | Adds medication/reminder | Focused | Too many fields | Short progressive form |
| Coordinate | Assigns a task | Relieved | Ownership is ambiguous | Explicit assignee + status |
| Execute | Marks task complete | Reassured | App may be slow to update | One-tap completion |
| Review | Checks week | Calm | Raw history is noisy | Simple completion insights |

## Moment that matters
The highest-value interaction is the **Today view**: it should answer “what, for whom, when, and who owns it?” within seconds.
