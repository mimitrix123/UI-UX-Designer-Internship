# Week 2 Project Overview — ShopEase

## Challenge
Design a complete e-commerce mobile app that lets users discover products, evaluate them, purchase securely, and manage their account without unnecessary friction.

## Target users
1. **Priya, 22, student:** price-sensitive, searches on mobile, wants quick checkout and saved wishlists.
2. **Arjun, 31, professional:** values reliable delivery, order tracking, and a clean search/filter experience.

## Design principles
- Make product discovery fast.
- Show price, rating, availability, and delivery information clearly.
- Keep checkout focused and transparent.
- Make repeat purchases and order tracking easy.
- Keep account and privacy controls understandable.

## Success metrics
Search-to-product click-through, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, wishlist saves, repeat purchase rate, and task-completion time.
