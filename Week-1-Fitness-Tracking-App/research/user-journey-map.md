# User Journey Map — First Week With FitTrack

| Stage | Discover | Sign Up | Choose Workout | Complete Workout | Review Progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| User goal | Find a simple app | Get started quickly | Pick the right session | Finish without confusion | Know whether it worked |
| Actions | Opens app/store | Enters basic info | Browses recommended sessions | Follows routine | Checks summary |
| Thoughts | “Will this be easy?” | “Don't make me fill too much.” | “Which one fits today?” | “How much is left?” | “Am I improving?” |
| Emotion | Curious | Cautious | Slightly uncertain | Motivated | Rewarded |
| Pain points | Too many options | Long onboarding | Unclear difficulty | Complex controls | Dense charts |
| Opportunities | Clear value proposition | Minimal onboarding | Recommendation + duration | Large controls + progress | Simple weekly insights |

## Key Moments
- **First 30 seconds:** establish value and a clear next action.
- **Workout selection:** remove decision paralysis.
- **Workout completion:** celebrate the accomplishment.
- **Progress review:** make improvement understandable.

## Design Response
The five wireframes directly address these moments through a simple dashboard, recommended workout cards, focused active-workout controls, a weekly progress view, and flexible goals/profile settings.
