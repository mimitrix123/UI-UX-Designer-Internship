# UX Research — FitTrack Fitness App

## Problem Statement
Many beginner and casual fitness users want to exercise consistently but struggle with motivation, planning, and understanding progress. Existing fitness apps can feel data-heavy or require too much setup.

## UX Goals
- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Reduce friction before starting a workout.
- Show progress in motivating, understandable language.
- Support realistic goals rather than perfection.
- Keep the first experience simple enough for beginners.

## Target Users
- Busy adults who want short, achievable workouts.
- Beginners who need guidance and encouragement.
- Casual exercisers who want lightweight progress tracking.

## Key Assumptions to Validate
1. Users prefer short workouts they can start immediately.
2. A daily progress summary is more motivating than a dense analytics dashboard.
3. Users need flexible goals because schedules change.
4. Visible streaks and weekly progress can encourage consistency.
5. Beginners benefit from clear workout difficulty and duration labels.

## Proposed Research Questions
- What prevents users from exercising regularly?
- Which fitness metrics do users actually understand and value?
- How long are users willing to spend setting up a workout?
- What makes progress feel motivating rather than judgmental?
- When do users want reminders?

## Primary User Needs
- Quick workout discovery
- Clear duration/difficulty
- Simple activity summary
- Goal setting
- Progress feedback
- Encouragement without pressure

## Design Principles
1. **Simple first:** prioritize the next action over secondary data.
2. **Progress over perfection:** frame missed days constructively.
3. **Scannable:** use clear hierarchy and familiar labels.
4. **Low cognitive load:** avoid unnecessary configuration.
5. **Accessible:** readable type, clear controls, strong contrast, and large touch targets.

## Success Metrics
- Time to start first workout
- Workout completion rate
- Weekly active users
- Goal-setting completion
- 7-day retention
- Self-reported ease of use
