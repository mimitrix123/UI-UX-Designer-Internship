# Persona 2 — Rahul Mehta

**Age:** 34  
**Occupation:** Sales manager  
**Fitness level:** Intermediate  
**Goal:** Maintain fitness while traveling and managing an unpredictable schedule

## Behaviors
- Exercises 3–4 times per week when possible.
- Alternates between walking, strength training, and cardio.
- Checks weekly progress rather than detailed daily statistics.
- Often has schedule changes.

## Frustrations
- Rigid plans don't fit his work travel.
- Too many notifications become annoying.
- Wants to compare progress across weeks.
- Doesn't want to manually enter everything.

## Motivations
- Maintaining stamina
- Staying consistent during travel
- Seeing measurable improvement
- Flexible planning

## Needs
- Flexible workout recommendations
- Weekly progress view
- Goal adjustment
- Minimal manual input

## Quote
> “My plan changes every week, so the app needs to adapt with me.”
