# Persona 1 — Ananya Sharma

**Age:** 24  
**Occupation:** Junior software professional  
**Fitness level:** Beginner  
**Goal:** Build a consistent 20–30 minute exercise habit

## Behaviors
- Works long hours at a desk.
- Exercises inconsistently.
- Uses her phone for most planning and tracking.
- Prefers guided routines over creating workouts herself.

## Frustrations
- Doesn't know which workout to choose.
- Loses motivation when she misses a day.
- Finds advanced fitness dashboards confusing.
- Has limited time on weekdays.

## Motivations
- More energy
- Better routine
- Visible progress
- Short workouts that fit her schedule

## Needs
- Quick-start workouts
- Time and difficulty shown clearly
- Friendly reminders
- Positive progress feedback

## Quote
> “I want something I can start in a few taps, not another complicated task.”
